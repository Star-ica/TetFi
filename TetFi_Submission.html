Project Blurb
TetFi is a fully on-chain lottery protocol built on BOT Chain. It removes the opacity and custody risks of traditional lotteries by executing every draw, payout, and record directly on-chain via auditable smart contracts. Participants choose from three modes — Ticket (buy numbered entries), Pool (equal-odds for any contribution), and Raffle (capped ticket supply for guaranteed draw) — and receive winnings automatically in the same transaction as the draw. There are no withdrawal delays, no hidden fees beyond a published 5% platform rate, and no token required beyond the native BOT currency. TetFi is designed for the everyday BOT Chain user: connect a wallet, buy a ticket, win on-chain.
